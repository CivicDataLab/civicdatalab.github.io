Akriti Singh is a Data Analyst at CivicDataLab, working at the intersection of data, technology, and public interest. She works on the DOST initiative, focusing on open data, Indian language datasets, metadata, data quality, and making datasets more accessible and usable. Her work involves data cleaning, analysis, standardisation, metadata creation, and visualisation, with experience using Python, SQL, Power BI, and Apache Superset. Previously, she worked with Capgemini and has experience across data analytics and technology. She holds a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science. Beyond work, she enjoys painting and sketching, particularly creating colourful artwork inspired by characters, nature, and everyday ideas.
