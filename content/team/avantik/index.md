Avantik is an AI-DAN Fellow at CivicDataLab, working for the state of Himachal Pradesh and focusing on various aspects of language data research and identification. His work includes reaching out to various researchers and institutions in the state, collaborating with people working on the low-resource languages and scripts of Himachal Pradesh and facilitating digitisation of available AI-ready data. Avantik holds a Master’s degree in Linguistics from the University of Mumbai, along with a Masters in English Literature. His areas of expertise include Language documentation, Phonetics & Phonology, Historical Linguistics and Language Analysis. He has worked on descriptive grammar for less spoken languages and also teaches a course on Phonetics & Phonology at the University of Mumbai. He is passionate about languages and likes to learn & study different languages and accents. Beyond work, he spends his time exploring nature, cooking, and reading classical literature.
