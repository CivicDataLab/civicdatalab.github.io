Muskaan Negi is an environmental and forestry professional working at the intersection of Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR), climate action, environmental conservation, and community resilience. She holds a Bachelor’s degree in Forestry, a Master’s degree in Forestry/Environmental Sciences, and a PhD in Environmental Science, with her doctoral research focusing on invasive alien plant species, forest ecology, and Himalayan ecosystems. With experience spanning research, field implementation, and programme management, she has worked on forest fire prevention and mitigation, climate resilience, biodiversity conservation, ecological restoration, and community preparedness, while engaging with communities, government departments, and development stakeholders. Her expertise includes GIS and spatial analysis, species distribution and habitat suitability modelling, environmental research, community engagement, capacity building, and programme management. Muskaan is deeply passionate about environmental conservation and about bridging scientific research with on-ground implementation and policy, with a strong belief in translating evidence and data into practical, scalable solutions that strengthen disaster resilience, climate action, sustainable ecosystems, and environmental governance.
