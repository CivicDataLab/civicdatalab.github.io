Gaurangi is a Communications Fellow at CivicDataLab, working on communications and storytelling around public-interest technology, data and civic issues. Her work focuses on creating clear, accessible and engaging content for diverse audiences.

Her work involves content research, writing, curation and digital communications, with a focus on translating complex ideas into simple and compelling narratives. She is interested in how thoughtful communication can make information, data and public-interest work more accessible and meaningful to people.

Previously, Gaurangi gained experience in social media management and audience engagement through roles at Times Internet and Action India. She holds a Bachelor’s degree in Journalism and Mass Communication from IILM University, Greater Noida. 

Outside of work, she is a theatre enthusiast, who finds joy in cinema, music, and theatre - always looking for meaning in human stories and everyday experiences.
