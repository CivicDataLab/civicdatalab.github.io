context: >-
  Climate risks are intensifying across the Asia-Pacific region, yet investment
  in climate adaptation and resilience continues to fall short of what is
  needed. While governments, multilateral development banks, philanthropies and
  private investors are exploring new financing mechanisms, challenges remain in
  translating climate-risk evidence and adaptation priorities into viable
  investment opportunities. Bridging this gap requires stronger data systems,
  institutional capacity, coordinated financing approaches and locally grounded
  solutions that ensure investments reach the communities most at risk.




  In partnership with the [UNDP Regional Bureau for Asia and the Pacific](https://www.undp.org/asia-pacific), and aligned with the Bangkok Climate Action Week (BKKCAW) 2026 theme, “New Economy, New Climate”, [CivicDataLab ](https://civicdatalab.in/) convened an expert roundtable titled “Financing Resilience: Unlocking Investment for Climate Adaptation and Regenerative Economies” at The Rockefeller Foundation, Bangkok, on 9 October 2026. \

  The discussion brought together government representatives, multilateral development banks, philanthropies, investors, development partners, researchers and civil society practitioners to explore how climate-risk data, digital technologies, public finance systems and cross-sector partnerships can help unlock investment for climate adaptation. The roundtable aimed to identify barriers, strengthen collaboration and explore pathways to translate climate-risk evidence into actionable investment priorities and inclusive, locally grounded resilience across the Asia-Pacific region.
