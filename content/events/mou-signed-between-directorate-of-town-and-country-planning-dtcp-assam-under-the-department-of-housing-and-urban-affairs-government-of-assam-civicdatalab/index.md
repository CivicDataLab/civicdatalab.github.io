context: >-
  CivicDataLab (CDL) has signed an MoU  with the Directorate of Town and Country
  Planning (DTCP), Assam, under the Department of Housing and Urban Affairs,
  Government of Assam, to strengthen institutional capacity for data-driven
  urban planning, climate risk management, and the use of emerging technologies
  in urban governance.


  The collaboration aims to enhance the state’s urban data infrastructure, with a particular focus on flood and heat risk management, integrating Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) capabilities into the existing GIS GeoHub Portal and supporting the establishment of a comprehensive Urban Data Observatory for Assam,  focusing on open-source climate indicators, including water scarcity, heatwaves, and urban flooding.


  Under the MoU, CivicDataLab will provide technical guidance on optimising the GIS GeoHub Portal, developing the Urban Data Observatory, and strengthening the use of open-source geospatial data for urban planning. The partnership will also facilitate technical knowledge exchange, stakeholder consultations, and institutional capacity-building programmes for government officials. Directorate of Town and Country Planning (DTCP) will support access to open-source geospatial datasets and facilitate engagement with relevant stakeholders through its events and capacity-building initiatives.


  The collaboration will further explore opportunities to strengthen climate adaptation and resilience through technical advisory support on green public finance and budgeting frameworks for relevant projects. It will also support the broader institutional ecosystem housed within the Directorate of Town and Country Planning (DTCP), including the Assam Urban Knowledge Hub (AUKH), the GIS GeoHub, and the Assam Climate Centre for Cities under the CITIIS programme.
