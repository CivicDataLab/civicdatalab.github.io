name: AI & Digital Tools for CSOs
summary: Building Practical Skills for Stronger Programmes and Evidence-Based Advocacy
context: Many Civil Society Organisations (CSOs) play an important role in
  addressing development challenges, but limited access to digital resources,
  technical expertise and AI awareness can restrict their ability to strengthen
  programmes and organisational processes. From documentation and proposal
  writing to data analysis, communication and workflow management, there is
  significant potential for AI and digital tools to make everyday operations
  more efficient. However, adopting these tools requires practical training,
  guidance on selecting appropriate solutions, and an understanding of
  responsible AI use, including data privacy, informed consent and bias
  awareness.
solution: >-
  Through the AI & Digital Tools for CSOs programme, CivicDataLab, in
  partnership with UNDP India, aims to equip approximately 100 CSO
  representatives across India with practical skills to integrate AI and digital
  tools into their organisational workflows.


  The programme will focus on four key learning areas: AI-assisted documentation and proposal writing, data analysis and visualisation, communication and outreach, and workflow management and collaboration. Through hands-on training, live demonstrations, mentoring clinics and peer learning, participants will explore accessible, open-source and free-to-use tools relevant to their day-to-day work.
