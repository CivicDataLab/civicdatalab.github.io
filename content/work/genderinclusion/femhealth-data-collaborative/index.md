summary: Strengthening Connected Data Systems for Better Health Outcomes for Women and Girls
context: >-
  India is home to over 696 million women, yet critical gaps remain in women’s
  health, including maternal and reproductive health, anaemia, cancer,
  malnutrition, adolescent pregnancy, mental health and gender-based violence.
  Fragmented datasets and limited interoperability across public systems
  constrain evidence-based policymaking and responsive health solutions for
  women and children.


  To address this, we are developing the FemHealth Data Collaborative, as part
  of the UNICEF Office of Innovation Femtech Ventures cohort, piloting an
  integrated data ecosystem for women’s health in Assam.


  It has also been formally accepted as a global commitment under the
  Commit2Data initiative, led by Open Data Watch and PARIS21 in the lead-up to
  the United Nations World Data Forum 2026. The initiative aims to strengthen
  inclusive and interoperable gender data ecosystems, promote responsible AI in
  governance, and advance evidence-based policymaking.
solution: >-
  Our Collaborative will bring together changemakers across intersections of
  women’s health and wellbeing to access, analyse and contribute critical
  datasets to better understand and address issues related to women and girls
  wellbeing in Assam. We strive to bring together changemakers across sectors to
  analyse & publish datasets, collate data use-cases, apply Artificial
  Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) techniques, and share knowledge
  for improving women’s health outcomes and mobilise resources for the most
  urgent needs in Assam. The platform will be open source and modular, built on
  scalable architecture.
